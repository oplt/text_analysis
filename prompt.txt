I re-audited the latest `main` commit, **`e94f241253e6de1ff1c8f65acb7ec862836ff249` (`r engine bugs`)**, against the completion criteria from the previous prompt.

The previous work closed most of the architectural gaps: multi-input identity is now propagated to Python, engine resolution is centralized, R worker liveness is per-worker, comparison orchestration is event-driven, DFM comparisons understand incomplete previews, R artifacts are copied out of temporary work directories, the R worker has cgroup limits, and the frontend distinguishes `not_ready` from `unavailable`.

It is **not yet complete**, primarily because current HEAD still has two failing mandatory CI gates, plus several deeper production/research gaps.
